How it works

Your numbers go to a Colab backend that predicts and logs them to GitHub. If Colab is offline, the page switches to a copy of the model in your browser and sends nothing.

Learn from sessions

200 decision trees each study a random resample of 30 recorded workouts, then vote on an answer.

Predict your burn

Your four numbers go through every tree. The average of their answers is your calorie estimate.

Show the error

We test the model by leaving out each session in turn. The typical miss is – kcal, so every result comes with a likely range.

Estimates only, based on a small dataset. Not medical advice.
